Off Balance Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Off Balance is a stacking game for Android. This policy explains what information the app collects and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

Offline play does not require an account. The app does not request an email address, phone number, password, contact list, or precise location. Friends and online scores use an anonymous Firebase account and a username you choose.

The app may collect:

  • A random account identifier used to protect your online records.
  • Your chosen public username and tower records by difficulty.
  • Local game progress, settings, and saved rivals.
  • Previously acquired subscription entitlement status, where applicable.
  • Basic app usage analytics, such as app opens, when collection is enabled.
  • Crash and diagnostic data if crash reporting is enabled.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Save your records and compare them with players you choose to follow.
  • Reserve your username and prevent other players from editing your profile.
  • Send play reminders if you enable them and grant notification permission.
  • Preserve existing purchases and diagnose reliability problems.

Sharing

Off Balance uses service providers to operate the app, including Google Firebase, Google Play, and Google Play Billing. These providers may process data according to their own terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information.

Your online username and scores can be found by other signed-in players. Saved rivals stay on your device. Tilt sensor readings are used locally for gameplay and are not uploaded. They are not sampled while the game is paused or the app is in the background.

Remote Configuration

Firebase Remote Config controls available features, optional offers, celebration timing, and reminder frequency. It uses installation and configuration data. It does not override your reminder permission or change gameplay during a run. Aggregate usage events may include completed runs, layer counts, difficulty, celebrations, and offer views. These events do not include usernames, purchase tokens, or payment details.

Payments

When available, the optional Style Pack unlocks cosmetic themes and extra music through Google Play. The app sends a purchase token and product identifier to a protected server to verify and restore the purchase. Verified entitlements are cached on your device. Purchase records are retained for verification, restoration, and refund handling. The app does not receive or store full payment instrument details.

Data Retention

Online profiles retain your best records and the current daily records. You can delete your public profile from the username screen; this removes its username reservation and scores. Local records remain until you clear app data or uninstall. Android may back up local settings and progress according to your device backup settings. Earlier version records and service diagnostics may be retained to provide support and prevent abuse.

Children's Privacy

Off Balance is not designed for children under 13. The Google Play target audience selection should match the final store listing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Google Play store listing for Off Balance.
