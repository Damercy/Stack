# Asset provenance

The geometric scene is drawn in Compose without external textures or models.
The original electro loops are generated by `scripts/generate_grooves.py` and
contain no sampled recordings. Legacy impact tones are generated by
`scripts/generate_sound_palettes.mjs`.

- Anton: https://github.com/googlefonts/AntonFont — SIL Open Font License.
- Roboto Mono: https://github.com/googlefonts/RobotoMono — SIL Open Font License.
- JBox2D: https://github.com/jbox2d/jbox2d — BSD 2-Clause.

The complete license notices are bundled in `licenses/`.
