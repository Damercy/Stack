# Asset provenance


Material and lighting sources:

- Stone 01, Poly Haven, CC0: https://polyhaven.com/a/stone_01
- Paper 001, ambientCG, CC0: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper001
- Leather 038, ambientCG, CC0: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Leather038
- Fabric 062, ambientCG, CC0: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric062
- White Home Studio, Poly Haven, CC0: https://polyhaven.com/a/white_home_studio

The packaged GLBs and HDR are optimized derivatives generated by
`scripts/generate_3d_assets.py`. Sound palettes are generated by
`scripts/generate_sound_palettes.mjs`.

River-stone textures are now deterministic procedural textures generated in the
script, with a restrained mineral vein. The Poly Haven stone reference is retained
above as historical provenance; its fractured surface maps are not packaged in
the current river-stone GLBs. Texture maps are capped at 512 pixels and geometry
is reduced for the bounded mobile renderer.
